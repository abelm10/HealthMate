About HealthMate

by Abel Mathews, HealthMate is a smart, rule-based health awareness web application designed to provide early guidance based on common symptoms.

The system does not diagnose diseases or prescribe medication.

This project demonstrates frontend–backend integration, all kinds of reviews are welcome, let me know how i can improve this better

⚠️ Always consult a qualified medical professional for serious or persistent symptoms.
